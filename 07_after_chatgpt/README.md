# After ChatGPT
This module is aimed to help us follow up the rapid emergence and disappearance of various AI ideas, technologies, and products, without chasing down the rabbit hole in endless dictionary-checking of tech words. This reading-only module walks us through how to analyze a new tech word, and then address a specific limit case called "AI hallucination". Then, we briefly talk about the consequence of having to "rent" an access to use big AI products.

## What You'll learn
* The four questions to raise seeing a new tech word.  
* What is hallucination and how it raises
* What is some consequences, since AI products are not owned.

## Take-Home Messages
* The four questions:
> 1. What is the background that this word emerges in the AI world? What is the limitation or gap to address?
> 2. How does the new concept respond to the existing gap?
> 3. What is the core arrangement underneath, stated in plain language and apart from the branding?
> 4. What should you be careful about when you see it?

* AI hallucination raises because GenAI only produces sequences of next word/partial word/phrase, that the output can be false, but not limited to untrue facts.

* Impossibility of ownership and its consequences are old issues in the new era.
